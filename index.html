About me

Turning ideas

I'm Alex Rowe, a creative developer and UI/UX designer based in New York. I specialize in building products that feel as good as they function — where every interaction has intention behind it.

With a background spanning web development, brand identity, and interactive media, I bridge the gap between design and engineering to deliver work that stands out in a crowded digital landscape.

When I'm not pushing pixels, you'll find me exploring generative art, contributing to open source, or chasing the perfect espresso.
